Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production texture for the BACK of a single playing card in a 3D cribbage game.
Primary request: create a beautiful classic American engraved playing card back, in the spirit of traditional Bicycle rider-back decks, with an original composition. Deep burgundy red ink and fine ivory-white engraved linework. Two reciprocal circular bicycle-wheel medallions, one in the top half and an identical inverted one in the bottom half, with small classical winged rider figures, surrounded by elegant acanthus foliage, scrolling vines, rosettes, and finely controlled cross-hatching. A rich nineteenth-century engraved, professionally printed design, intricate and confident, not a modern geometric pattern.
Composition/framing: a FLAT, straight-on, orthographic, print-ready full-card texture, portrait ratio 768:1116 (approximately 0.688:1). The image canvas is exactly the rectangle of the card itself, no surrounding scene, no outer padding beyond the card. The complete design has a single clean white paper border about 3.5% of the card width on all four sides, with a restrained double-line burgundy frame inside it. Square image corners; the game supplies rounded physical corners. Very carefully balanced two-way 180-degree rotational symmetry. The lower half is the exact rotated counterpart of the upper half; no way to tell which end is up. Fine linework must still form a coherent, beautiful back at thumbnail size.
Materials/textures: crisp matte red ink on clean white card stock, clear linework, no simulated lighting or embossing.
Constraints: one card back only, full design visible. No title, no lettering, no brand name, no logos, no numbers, no suit indices, no watermark. No mockup, no camera perspective, no tilt, no shadows, no card thickness, no tabletop, no hands, no gradients from lighting. Do not draw a stack of cards. Fill the entire canvas with this single print-ready card-back texture.
