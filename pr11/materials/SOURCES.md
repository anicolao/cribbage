# Material assets

These locally bundled scans are from Poly Haven, licensed CC0.

- `maple-*.jpg`: [White Maple Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_maple_veneer), 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness.
- `walnut-*.jpg`: [American Walnut Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/american_walnut_veneer), 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness.
- `cloth-*.jpg`: [Fabric Pattern 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07), 2K OpenGL normal and roughness. Fine-scale surface detail is used with the game's green baize material; the patterned cotton albedo is not used.
- `studio.hdr`: [Studio Small 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09), 1K HDR environment.

[Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license)

Original files were downloaded using metadata from the public Poly Haven API. Material colours, scan scale, normal strength, satin coating, and fibre sheen are adjusted in the scene. The board's markings are composited over scanned maple at runtime.
