BAIZE AT DUSK
An original 48-bar pastorale in D major, 6/8, for flute, violin and violoncello.
About 103 seconds. The contrasting middle section gives the violin the melody;
the flute returns above a gently moving cello line. The chamber tail wraps into
the opening for repeat playback.

Composition, arrangement, MIDI, MusicXML and rendering scripts: GPL-3.0-only,
as part of the Cribbage repository. The notes are in scores/baize-at-dusk.json.
Recreate the notation and MIDI with bun run music:compose. Render the recording
with bun run music:render and CRIBBAGE_FFMPEG pointing to ffmpeg if necessary.

Acoustic instrument recordings: VSCO 2 Community Edition, Versilian Studios /
Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell, CC0 1.0. The cello part uses a small section.
Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
Publisher: https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/

The upstream license is retained in VSCO-LICENSE.txt. Original source samples

Paper, wooden peg and table effects are original procedural sounds, synchronized
with the actual card and peg motions. They have a separate volume control.
